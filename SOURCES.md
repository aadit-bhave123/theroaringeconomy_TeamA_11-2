## Notes on historical methodology

- Compare contemporary records with later interpretations.
- Avoid treating marketing claims as proof of actual purchasing power.
- The 1920 Census was the first U.S. census reporting a majority urban population; 51.2 percent is a commonly reported figure under the definitions of that census.
- The 1919 strike wave should not be confused with Red Summer, the term associated with racial violence.
- Model T assembly-line methods were pioneered before the 1920s and helped drive growth during that decade.
- Teaching simulations use fictional numbers and are not historical datasets.
